Open questions

Open questions

Open from the SPA migration (ADR 0015), below. D1–D11 and the product questions raised during review are recorded in 08-decisions.md, with the settled answers folded into the chapters they affect.

  1. Rate limit keying. Open-name guests share one API bucket per address (burst 1000); at a venue behind one Wi-Fi address, or with SB_TRUSTED_PROXIES unset, all attendees share it. Recommendation: key by address plus guest cookie with a cap per address (the cookie is unsigned, so any id buys a fresh bucket).
  2. Comment likes. The SPA has neither the “Liked by” hover preview nor the avatars the legacy comments show. Recommendation: add both when comments are next touched; the data is in the snapshot already.
  3. A toast in the SPA. Saving or deleting a session still lands on /legacy/{slug}: a hard navigation drops the confirmation toast, which is client state. Recommendation: give the SPA its own toast, then move the session forms.
  4. server/ reaching app/. utils/guest-clashes.ts imports @/app/(site)/session_utils, which server-does-not-reach-into-app does not catch. Recommendation: move the two helpers to packages/domain.
  5. A session that loses its only room reads moved to No location (was Room A) in notices. Recommendation: reword to “no longer has a location (was Room A)”.
  6. User menu roles copy the legacy markup without arrow-key support. Recommendation: use the ARIA menu pattern with arrow keys when the menu is next changed.
  7. Flaky E2E. scheduling.spec edit, delete and cancel flows flake about one run in four at 8 workers (the retry sees the first attempt’s leftover session); settings.spec:67 can abort its first goto; attendee-count.spec after an SPA to legacy hop stays red unless the spec starts on /legacy/.... Recommendation: move the scheduling forms into the SPA, which removes the hop, and keep each attempt’s trace (--trace=retain-on-failure) to find the rest.
  8. Picking a name without being a member. The SPA picker offers only the snapshot’s guests (members and hosts), because GET /guests is closed to anonymous visitors, so a non-member cannot pick their name. Recommendation: a public guest-name list for the picker that exposes names only.

Add new questions here as numbered items with a recommendation; when one is decided, move the outcome into the relevant chapter and, if it is a design decision rather than a product detail, into 08-decisions.md.
